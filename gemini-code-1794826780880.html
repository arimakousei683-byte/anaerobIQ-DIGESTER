Core System Modules

Summary of the four connected modules documented in the Biogas Suite v7.7 User Manual.

Module 01

Digester Calculator

Calculates cow manure, food waste, and garden waste masses alongside a 1:1 solids-to-water slurry ratio, target C:N ratios (25.0:1, 27.5:1, 30.0:1), and cooking yield estimates.

Module 02

AI Planner

Generates mixture shares and a four-stage production timeline (Hydrolysis, Acidogenesis, Peak Methanogenesis, Stabilization) across 15–45 day retention cycles.

Module 03

pH Estimator

Models buffer capacity (Low, Moderate, High) and simulated reagent dosage splits based on digester volume, slurry volume, and current vs. target pH levels.

Module 04

3D CAD Simulator

Visualizes the reactor vessel with Cutaway X-Ray mode, ball-valve flow states, biogas storage bag telemetry, and virtual stove burner diagnostics.

Simulation & Safety Notice

Outputs generated by anaerobIQ DIGESTER (Biogas Suite v7.7) are software-modeled estimates for planning and educational visualization. Do not treat simulated pH dosages, gas pressures, or virtual burner controls as instructions for handling physical chemicals or operating unvalidated biogas equipment.
